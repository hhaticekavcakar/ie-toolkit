Rounding

Safety stock and reorder point are rounded up to whole units: rounding down would give less protection than the chosen service level. EOQ is rounded to the nearest whole unit; total cost changes very little near the optimum, so a convenient pack size close to the EOQ is fine.

Assumptions

  • Daily demand is roughly normally distributed and independent from day to day.
  • Lead time is constant; only demand varies.
  • Service level is the probability of not running out during one replenishment cycle, not the share of demand filled.
  • EOQ assumes steady demand, fixed ordering and holding costs, and no quantity discounts.
